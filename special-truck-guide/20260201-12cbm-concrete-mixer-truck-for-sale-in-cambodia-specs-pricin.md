## Field Notes

Customs problems rarely start at the port; they usually begin with inconsistent paperwork. The most important documents are the commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and export declaration. Depending on the destination, buyers may also need inspection certificates, emission compliance papers, and vehicle title documents. If these do not match the chassis number, engine number, and invoice details exactly, clearance can stall for weeks.

Container shipping is suitable for smaller mixer trucks or knocked-down units, especially when buyers want better protection from salt spray and theft. In some cases, an 8 cbm mixer truck can be partially disassembled and loaded into a 40-foot high cube container. However, container loading requires experienced packing, and not every model fits efficiently. Poor packing is a frequent source of hidden damage and delayed unloading.
