## FAQ

**Q: how to operate truck mounted crane**

Operating a truck mounted crane requires proper training, pre-use inspection, and strict adherence to the manufacturer’s manual and local safety regulations. Before operation, confirm the truck is parked on firm, level ground, outriggers are fully deployed, and the load chart is understood. Inspect hydraulic systems, cables, hooks, controls, tires, and the boom for any damage or leaks. During lifting, keep personnel clear of the load, never exceed rated capacity, and move the crane smoothly and slowly to avoid swinging. Use a qualified signal person when visibility is limited. If you need the correct operating procedure for a specific model, please share the truck type and crane capacity.

**Q: how to wire a semi trailer plug**

Wiring a semi trailer plug depends on the plug type, trailer lighting system, and the tractor’s pin configuration, but the process is generally straightforward for a qualified technician. First, confirm whether you are using a 7-way or 7-pin connector and follow the standard wiring diagram for your region. Typically, the wires are connected to ground, tail lights, left and right turn/brake lights, auxiliary power, and ABS or reverse circuits. Use proper gauge wire, weatherproof connectors, and secure grounding to avoid intermittent faults. If you need the correct harness or replacement plug, prices usually range from about USD 15 to USD 80, depending on quality and configuration.
