## FAQ

**Q: who makes the best quality dump trailer**

Match the specification to the job, not the brochure. For a special truck: confirm body capacity against your per-day volume, check axle rating against payload, and insist on a chassis brand with local parts support — Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW and ISUZU have the widest networks in Africa and Southeast Asia. Ask each supplier for the same specification sheet so you can compare price, delivery and warranty line by line.

**Q: do you need a cdl for farm use**

Generally yes, with one condition: buy from a real manufacturer, not the lowest quotation. A properly specced special truck handles commercial duty cycles well; the failures you hear about usually trace to overloaded cheap builds with no parts support. Ask for the factory license, shipment history and a written 12-month warranty, and use third-party inspection before the balance payment.

**Q: how to repair truck bed**

Repairing a truck bed depends on the damage type and material. For minor dents, cracks, or surface rust, you can often clean the area, sand it down, apply a metal filler or weld patch, then prime and repaint. If the bed is severely rusted, bent, or structurally damaged, replacement panels or a full bed replacement may be more cost-effective. For aluminum beds, use proper aluminum repair methods to avoid weakening the structure. For businesses, we recommend inspection by a qualified body shop or truck repair specialist to assess safety and durability. Typical repair costs range from about USD 200 for small cosmetic fixes to USD 3,000+ for major structural repair.
