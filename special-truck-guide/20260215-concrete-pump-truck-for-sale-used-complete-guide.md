This keeps risk low and ensures a clean paper trail for customs clearance.

## Field Notes

A premium manufacturer may cost 10%–20% more, but the pump stability, boom movement accuracy, and after-sales support are often better. Buyers who choose only by lowest price frequently discover hidden costs: faster wear on wearing parts, harder-to-source valves, and longer repair intervals. This becomes especially painful if the truck is used on commercial jobs where one missed pour can cost more than the parts savings.

For field use, overseas buyers should also think about road and climate conditions. In hot markets, cooling performance matters more than brochure horsepower. In dusty regions, air filtration and hydraulic sealing become critical. A cheaper chassis may save USD 8,000–15,000 upfront, but the extra maintenance and downtime can quickly erase that saving.
