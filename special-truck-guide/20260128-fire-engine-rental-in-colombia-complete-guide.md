## Field Notes

Many importers search how to choose low price fire engine because they want fast deployment with limited capital. That can work, but only if the buyer understands what is being reduced. Lower-cost trucks may use a simpler chassis, smaller pump, fewer accessories, or a less advanced control system. For some markets, that is acceptable. For others, it creates hidden costs through downtime, spare parts shortages, and higher fuel use.

For practical reference, a standard China-made new fire engine with a 3,000 to 6,000 liter water tank, 1,500 to 3,000 L/min pump, and a 120 to 220 hp chassis often falls around USD 45,000 to 95,000 depending on brand, drivetrain, and equipment level. Higher-spec airport or industrial units can go well above USD 150,000. When buyers search for how to choose new fire engine, the most important question is not “what is available?” but “what operational gap must this truck fill?”
