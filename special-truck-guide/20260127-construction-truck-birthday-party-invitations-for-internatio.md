## Field Notes

When evaluating an LHD construction truck how to choose, buyers should check whether the truck will work on narrow worksites, how often it enters public roads, and whether the operator is trained for local traffic conditions. For an RHD construction truck buying guide, the key issue is not just steering position but also parts access and dashboard layout. Some buyers overlook mirror angle, wiper sweep, and instrument placement, only to discover visibility problems during night hauling or rainy-season operations.

The biggest mistake is choosing a truck by gross tonnage alone. Two trucks with the same payload can behave very differently if one has better suspension, stronger axles, or a more suitable wheelbase for local roads.
