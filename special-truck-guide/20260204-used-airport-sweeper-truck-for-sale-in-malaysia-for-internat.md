In practical terms, many buyers look for a LHD airport sweeper truck for export because left-hand drive matches most airport fleets in Africa, the Middle East, Latin America, and parts of Europe. RHD airport sweeper truck delivery time can be longer if the unit needs special steering conversion or a non-standard production slot. For customs clearance, the documents must be accurate and consistent.
