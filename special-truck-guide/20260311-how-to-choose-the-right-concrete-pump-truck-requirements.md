For medium projects, **SHACMAN concrete pump truck 20m specifications** are popular because they balance maneuverability and reach. A realistic setup may include a 6x4 chassis, 20 m boom, 30 to 40 MPa pumping pressure, and a theoretical output of 80 to 110 m3/h. Buyers should also check hopper capacity, pipe diameter, and whether the delivery cylinder is wear-resistant alloy steel. In overseas projects, these details matter more than nominal boom length because poor material selection increases downtime quickly.

The real cost of a concrete pump truck is not only the purchase price. Overseas buyers should calculate fuel consumption, spare parts availability, wear parts, and service intervals over three to five years. A well-configured unit may use fewer replacement elbows, sealing rings, and pipeline sections, saving significant money. In many markets, a lower-priced pump becomes more expensive once downtime and imported parts are added.
