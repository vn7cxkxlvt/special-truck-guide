## FAQ

**Q: how dangerous is truck driving statistics**

Truck driving can be hazardous, but the level of danger depends heavily on training, vehicle condition, route type, and compliance with safety rules. In the U.S., large truck crashes account for a small share of total traffic accidents, but they can lead to more severe injuries due to vehicle size and weight. According to recent safety data, thousands of fatal truck-related crashes occur each year, with fatigue, speeding, distraction, and improper loading among the main risk factors. For commercial fleets, strong maintenance programs, driver training, and telematics can significantly reduce risk and improve operational safety.

Generally yes, with one condition: buy from a real manufacturer, not the lowest quotation. **Q: can you tow car with uhaul truck**

**Q: how much trash can a garbage truck hold**

For most overseas buyers a garbage truck lands between $28,500 and $235,000 FOB China.
