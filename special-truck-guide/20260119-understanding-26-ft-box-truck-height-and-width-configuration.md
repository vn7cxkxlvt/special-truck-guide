## Field Notes

A typical new 3.5-ton to 8-ton box truck from China may cost around USD 18,000 to 45,000, depending on chassis brand, cargo box length, insulation level, and refrigeration equipment. Refrigerated versions are usually higher, often USD 28,000 to 65,000 or more, especially when fitted with a reliable cooling unit and thicker insulation panels. Common specifications include payloads from 2 to 10 tons, box lengths from 3.8 to 7.8 meters, diesel engines, and manual or automatic transmissions. For special-duty applications, buyers sometimes also ask about items like a “logger box truck bed for sale,” but for overseas imports it is important to confirm whether the truck body is for general cargo, frozen food, or a specialized industry.

After selecting a supplier, confirm the exact configuration in writing. This should include box material, floor thickness, insulation thickness, door type, refrigeration brand, and optional items such as liftgate or side door. Then sign a proforma invoice and pay the agreed deposit, usually 30% to 50%. Production time for a new truck is often 20 to 45 days, while ready-stock used units may be shipped faster.
