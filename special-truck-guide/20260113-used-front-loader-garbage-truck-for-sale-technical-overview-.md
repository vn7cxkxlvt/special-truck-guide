An anti-collision system in cars is a safety technology designed to help prevent or reduce the severity of accidents. It uses sensors, radar, cameras, or lidar to monitor the vehicle’s surroundings and detect obstacles, other vehicles, pedestrians, or sudden hazards. If a collision risk is identified, the system may warn the driver, automatically apply the brakes, reduce engine power, or assist with steering. In special trucks and commercial vehicles, anti-collision systems are especially valuable because of larger blind spots, heavier loads, and longer stopping distances. These systems improve road safety, reduce downtime, and help protect both drivers and cargo.
