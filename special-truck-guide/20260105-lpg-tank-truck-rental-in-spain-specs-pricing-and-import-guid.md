**Q: how to choose small mining dump truck**

To choose a small mining dump truck, start with your mine conditions and hauling needs. Check payload capacity, engine power, gradeability, turning radius, and ground clearance to match your site roads, slope, and load weight. For narrow tunnels or tight working areas, compact dimensions and strong maneuverability are important. Also review axle strength, brake performance, fuel efficiency, and maintenance access, since mining environments are demanding. If the truck will work in soft or rough ground, choose heavy-duty tires and a reinforced frame. Finally, compare after-sales support, spare parts availability, and warranty. A suitable small mining dump truck typically costs about USD 25,000–80,000, depending on configuration and capacity.
