**Q: what is a ladder fire truck**

A ladder fire truck is a specialized fire apparatus equipped with an extendable aerial ladder or platform used by firefighters to reach upper floors, rooftops, and elevated rescue points during emergencies. It is typically used for firefighting, rescue operations, ventilation, and access to tall buildings where ground-level entry is not enough. These trucks usually carry important equipment such as hoses, pumps, water tanks, and rescue tools. In the market, a ladder fire truck can vary widely in price depending on ladder height, chassis, customization, and new or used condition, typically ranging from about USD 300,000 to over USD 1,200,000.
