## Field Notes

A real factory audit should go beyond a showroom visit. The most useful checks are practical: material traceability, welding process control, axle and suspension sourcing, and whether the plant has repeat export experience for your market. If the manufacturer cannot show batch records for steel, axles, kingpins, and brake systems, that is a warning sign.

Field experience also shows that good suppliers are usually transparent about what they do not make themselves. For example, many reliable Chinese trailer plants assemble with trusted components from BPW, FUWA, SAF, JOST, WABCO, or similar brands, while controlling frame fabrication and final inspection internally. That is often a healthier model than a “one-price-fits-all” exporter with no real production depth.
