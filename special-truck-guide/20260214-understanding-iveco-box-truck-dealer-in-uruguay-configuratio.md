**Q: what is a carrier truck**

A special truck is a purpose-built special purpose vehicle mounted on a truck chassis, engineered for daily commercial duty rather than general haulage. Chinese export versions typically use Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a specialized body, and are supplied with FOB pricing, CE or destination-market certificates and 12-month warranties. Most units ship by RO-RO or flat-rack container with lead times of 30-60 days.

**Q: what is sanitation truck**

A sanitation truck is a special-purpose vehicle used for collecting, transporting, and disposing of waste or recyclable materials. It is commonly used by municipalities, waste management companies, and industrial facilities for daily garbage collection and sanitation services. Depending on the application, sanitation trucks may include rear loaders, side loaders, front loaders, vacuum trucks, or compactors. They are designed for efficiency, hygiene, and safe handling of waste. If you are considering purchase, prices typically vary by configuration, capacity, and chassis brand, but a realistic range is about USD 50,000 to USD 180,000 per unit.
