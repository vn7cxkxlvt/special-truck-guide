## FAQ

**Q: what is the best way to clean cement dust**

The best way to clean cement dust is to use a combination of dry removal and controlled wet cleaning. First, vacuum loose dust with an industrial vacuum equipped with a HEPA filter to prevent spreading fine particles. Avoid sweeping, as it can put dust back into the air. For surfaces, use a damp microfiber cloth or mop with clean water or a mild neutral cleaner. For equipment or truck parts, low-pressure washing may be suitable, followed by thorough drying to prevent corrosion. If the dust is heavy or hardened, use specialized dust extraction or pressure washing systems designed for construction equipment.

**Q: how to garbage trucks work**

Garbage trucks work by collecting waste from bins or containers and compacting it inside the vehicle to maximize load capacity. Typically, the truck stops at each pickup point, and the crew or automated arm lifts the container and empties it into a hopper. A hydraulic compaction system then compresses the waste into the main body of the truck. Some models use rear loading, side loading, or front loading depending on the route and collection method. When full, the truck transports the waste to a landfill, transfer station, or recycling facility, where the load is discharged. These trucks are built for heavy-duty, frequent stop-and-go operation.
