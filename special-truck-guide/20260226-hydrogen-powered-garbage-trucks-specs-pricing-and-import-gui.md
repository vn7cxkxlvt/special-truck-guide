## FAQ

**Q: how to choose new deicing truck**

To choose a new deicing truck, start by matching the truck to your operational needs: airport size, annual deicing volume, climate severity, and required spray reach. Check tank capacity, fluid heating system, nozzle control, boom or platform design, chassis reliability, and winter performance. Make sure the truck complies with local aviation and environmental standards and can operate efficiently at your busiest temperatures. Also consider operator comfort, maintenance access, spare parts availability, and after-sales service. If you need multiple units, standardizing on one model can reduce training and maintenance costs. New deicing trucks typically range from about USD 180,000 to 600,000, depending on configuration and automation level.
