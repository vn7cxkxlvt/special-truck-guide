## Field Notes

When sourcing from China, the best choice depends on project type, local regulations, and budget. If your market needs compact municipal service vehicles, a light-duty model is usually enough. If you need a machine for multi-shift engineering maintenance, a medium-duty truck offers the best balance of cost and function. For harsh environments or heavy industrial contracts, a heavy-duty configuration is more suitable.

Pay attention to these technical points:
- generator output, usually 5 kW to 30 kW
- welding current range, commonly 160A to 500A
- body material, often steel or aluminum alloy
- optional devices such as compressor, crane, lift, or hydraulic ram
