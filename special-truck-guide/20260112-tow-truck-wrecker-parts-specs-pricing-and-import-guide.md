# Tow Truck Wrecker Parts: Specs, Pricing and Import Guide

> 清障车 配件

Pricing for a tow truck wrecker parts varies significantly based on chassis origin, body capacity, and specification level. Chinese chassis builds offer the best value, while Japanese (ISUZU, Hino) and European (MAN, Volvo, Scania) options command premiums of 25% to 65%. Ocean freight by RO-RO or container adds $3,000 to $8,000 depending on destination port.

Proper maintenance of a tow truck wrecker parts centers on the PTO engagement system, hydraulic oil quality, and body mounting integrity. Most Chinese export models are designed for simple field maintenance with standard tools. A complete spare parts kit (filters, seals, hydraulic hoses) should be ordered with the initial purchase to ensure availability in remote operating locations.

The tow truck wrecker parts is typically configured with a power take-off (PTO) system driving the specialized equipment. Engine options range from 180 to 420 hp depending on capacity requirements, with 6-speed to 12-speed manual or AMT gearboxes. Emission standards range from Euro 3 to Euro 6 depending on the destination market's requirements.
