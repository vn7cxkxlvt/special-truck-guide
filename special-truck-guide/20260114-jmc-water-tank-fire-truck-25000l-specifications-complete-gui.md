## Field Notes

When evaluating suppliers, it is important to separate chassis providers from complete vehicle manufacturers. A reliable factory should be able to explain whether it produces the tank body, pump system, control panel, ladder or monitor, foam system, and final vehicle integration in-house, or whether it relies on subcontractors. Buyers should ask for clear technical drawings and recent export references.

Certifications are critical in overseas procurement. At minimum, buyers should request documentation for the vehicle chassis, pressure vessel or tank fabrication where applicable, and quality management systems. ISO 9001 is common, but it is not enough by itself. Depending on the destination country, you may also need road approval documents, emission compliance, pump performance data, and third-party inspection reports from SGS, BV, or a similar agency.
